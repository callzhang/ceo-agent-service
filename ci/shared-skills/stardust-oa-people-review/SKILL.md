# Stardust OA People Review

Use together with `$dingtalk-oa-approval` for Stardust hiring, resignation, promotion, hiring-requisition, probation-confirmation, compensation-change and org-structure-change approvals. This Skill groups related people evidence and review mechanics; each approval type retains its own criteria and gaps. Do not transfer a rule from one type to another. The generic Skill owns cross-company evidence, scoring, workflow, and DingTalk action mechanics.

Read the current, applicable HR/performance source when one exists. Confirm source title, version/effective date and scope; historical feedback and recruiting methodology may add context but cannot create a company-wide approval threshold or action authority.

## Hiring

Read every substantive item required for this candidate and role, including the trial/probation objectives, resume, complete interview record/evaluation, hiring-team supplement, salary basis and OA remarks. When a candidate link is absent, search the candidate/interview system using available identity details and inspect the candidate's complete interview context; a missing link alone is not proof the source material does not exist.

Check that:

- probation objectives are measurable and tied to real current problems for the role/team;
- interview evidence addresses the problems and demonstrates the claimed skills, potential and role fit;
- role level is checked against the current job-leveling source;
- proposed compensation fits the salary standard below;
- current department/business context supports the stated role need and probation plan.

**Salary standard** (Derek, 2026-09-23). HR keeps a unified salary table; when one exists and covers the role, the proposed salary and level must match it. When there is no HR table for the role, **do the market research yourself** (Derek, 2026-09-23): search current postings and salary data for the same role, city and level on BOSS直聘, 猎聘, 职友集 and similar sources, record each source, the band it shows and the date, and judge which tier of that band the candidate's actual demonstrated performance (interview evidence, track record) supports. Do not return the approval to HR merely because they did not attach a salary basis; the market check is our work. Return to the submitter only when the proposed salary or level does not fit the HR table or the market band and tier you found, naming the gap and the sources; this is a material gap, not a rule gap, and is not escalated to `needs_human`. With this standard, salary and level are covered: an HR table or local-market band plus a tier judgment is the complete rule, so a case that meets it can reach `rule_coverage = 1.0`.

**Material the submitter supplies goes back to the submitter** (Derek, 2026-09-23). Salary basis, market comparison, leveling rationale, probation objectives and interview records are submitted material: when one is missing, return the approval asking for it. When the submitted material contradicts itself or the process state — for example an interviewer who explicitly did not recommend the candidate while the candidate system already shows an offer — the submitter must explain it: return with both facts named and ask for the explanation and the decision record behind it (generic decision table row 6). When the submitter's explanation points to a record — a hiring round-table, a recruiting stand-up's AI minutes, an interview-system note — find and read that record yourself (`dws minutes` by title and date for minutes) before deciding; judge the contradiction on what it says. Return for a link only after searching by name, date and attendees fails, and say what was searched. Do not escalate such a contradiction to `needs_human`; only a gap in the company's own rules or authority is escalated, independently, under the generic Skill's two-way handling.

**When required hiring material has not been fully read, take no workflow action** (Derek, 2026-09-23). Do not approve, return or reject. Unread trial objectives or interview records are almost always a reading failure on our side, not something the applicant failed to supply: the records usually exist in the candidate/interview system. Returning the approval would send a candidate's hiring back through the process for our own gap. So first search the candidate/interview system with the candidate's identity and read the complete interview context. If a required item still cannot be read after that, keep the approval pending, comment asking for an accessible copy or link naming the exact item, and escalate `needs_human` stating what was searched and what is still unread. This is the generic rule for unread material (generic Skill principle 5) applied to hiring, where the records most often live in the interview system.

## Resignation

Read the handover document, OA remarks/comments, and records for the receiving person, accounts and company assets. Verify handover items are specific and traceable, the recipient has acknowledged acceptance, and there is a concrete account/access and asset transfer or closure arrangement. A statement such as “已给” without a link or itemized receipt is not auditable evidence; ask for a traceable handover list or record.

This Skill does not yet define the completion standard, time-critical exceptions, or the authority of HR/manager/asset and account owners. An incomplete handover is a material gap: return it for a traceable handover record under the decision table. Do not infer approval authority from role title or past cases; escalate the exact unresolved branch with `needs_human`.

### Explicit no-return instructions and target routing

When the latest OA comments or an explicit instruction from the current authorized approver says not to return the approval to a named person, that instruction overrides the generic return-to-submitter route: do not return the approval to that person. Do not treat the instruction as evidence that the handover gap is resolved. Keep the handover evidence checks above, and do not approve solely because returning to the named person is prohibited.

Before proposing another recipient, identify the exact unresolved handover item and who owns or can complete it. Route a correctable gap only to a valid OA target whose responsibility for that gap is supported by evidence. Do not assume the applicant, initiator, handover recipient, HR or an asset/account owner is the correct target based only on their title or presence in the workflow. If no such target is established, keep the current task pending with `no_action` and use `needs_human` for the unresolved routing decision. Do not ask that person to repeat already verified material.

OA comments and linked records are eligible evidence for handover receipt. Match each confirmation to its recipient and the specific handover item; a later substantive recipient confirmation supersedes an older ‘待确认’ entry for the same item. A generic `AGREE` node alone does not replace item-level evidence. Do not ask the applicant to repeat or reformat a confirmation already verified in the current process comments or linked records. If evidence covers only part of the handover, request only the unresolved item or missing detail; do not restate items already confirmed.

Resolve the commenter and the person referred to by an explicit no-return instruction against OA identities and the process roles before applying it; do not infer that ‘me’ means the applicant or initiator. The restriction excludes only the identified person. If the prohibited person is ambiguous, keep the task pending with `no_action` and use `needs_human` for the unresolved routing decision.

Account and credential offboarding may be scheduled for the last working day or a controlled post-approval cutover when the employee still needs access for active work. Before approval, verify the responsible owner, timing, and dependencies; do not require a future operation to be complete. The plan must be specific enough to identify who will act and when, and must not extend beyond an established offboarding deadline without an explicit mitigation or decision. For active shared or service credentials, use an owner-coordinated rotation and validation plan; do not demand immediate revocation that would interrupt active service. Do not put credential values in OA records; a redacted secret is not a missing handover item. After the scheduled action is due, verify its completion through the appropriate owner or system record. If no safe owner-and-timing plan is documented, identify only that gap for the person who can establish it, or escalate the policy/authority question with `needs_human`.

## Promotion

Read the full promotion plan, performance/history or feedback sources, meeting records, OA comments and attachments. Assess whether the evidence covers changed role responsibilities, demonstrated business outcomes, capability gaps, organizational collaboration, management methods, risk anticipation and measurable follow-up goals. Search current materials about the person, role and department to ground the proposed plan in actual needs.

The 2026 performance policy may inform performance evidence, but where it delegates grade/level change to other policies (including the leveling and promotion rules), retrieve those primary sources before applying them. Do not substitute role proposals or historical feedback for promotion policy.

The current source set does not establish eligibility thresholds, time-in-role, cross-level exceptions, leveling/salary adjustment authority, exception approvers or approve/return/reject mappings. Where these matter, `rule_coverage < 1.0`; use `needs_human` and name the missing primary policy/authority.

## Hiring requisition (招聘需求申请) (Derek, 2026-09-24; approval timing clarified 2026-10-13)

Recruitment approval is the **prerequisite** for the role to be created or appear in 小青. Before the OA approval, a missing Xiaoqing role, candidate pipeline or separate approved-HC field is expected; it is not a gap, contradiction, reason to return, or reason for `needs_human`. Do not require the post-approval recruiting record to approve the preceding requisition. Use the submitted requisition and its budget/project evidence, together with the responsible business-line owner's explicit OA decision on the requested number. If that owner approves the stated number and project/budget evidence is consistent, approve the requisition without requiring an independent XiaoQing HC record. The approval result then enables the hiring plan/role to appear in 小青.

Use the **hiring list** and **interview situation** in 小青 (`xiaoqing_interview` MCP) to track execution after approval and to review a specific candidate's hiring process. The list is the recruiting plan once the requisition has been approved (Derek: "招聘清单就是计划"). A prior Agent comment asking Derek to decide is not itself an outstanding decision after Derek or the responsible business-line owner has subsequently made that decision in the OA flow or current task instruction; reread the latest process state and apply the current decision.

Where it is: call `get_dashboard_stats` with `period=all` (it also takes `jobStatus`, `priority`, `recruiter`, `jobId` filters). `data.job_process` is one row per job: `job_id`, `job_title`, `priority` (P0/P1), `recruiter`, and stage counts `total_count`, `resume_qualified_count`, `interviewed_count`, `first_interview_count`, `second_interview_count`, `third_interview_count`, `written_test_count`, `offer_count`, `awaiting_entry_count`, `abandoned_entry_count`, `onboarded_count`, plus `progress_summary` (for example "等待二面 1人"). Read the interview situation for a role with `search_candidates` (`job_title`, `stage`, `round`) and `list_candidate_interviews`.

What the list does **not** contain: requested headcount, approved HC, remaining gap, target hires, or job open/closed status. `total_count` is the number of candidates, never a headcount. Do not treat it as one. The list is also scoped to what the logged-in user can see.

How to judge:
- For the **pre-approval requisition**, confirm the requested number in the OA form, the responsible business-line owner's decision in the OA flow, and consistency with available project and budget evidence. The business-line owner's approval of the stated number is sufficient business approval when those facts align; the absence of a Xiaoqing record before approval is not contrary evidence.
- Candidate count is never headcount and cannot establish approved HC. Do not use pipeline stage counts to infer or veto the requested number.
- If the responsible business-line owner has not decided, the number is disputed, or objective project/budget evidence conflicts, name that exact unresolved issue for the responsible decision maker.
- After approval, match the created recruiting role by `job_title` and use its pipeline to track execution. A post-approval pipeline mismatch is a recruiting follow-up issue; it does not retroactively make the pre-approval role's absence a reason to reject the requisition.

The recruiting list and interview situation are read by us, not material the applicant supplies. They are not a pre-approval gate. If a specific post-approval execution review requires Xiaoqing and the MCP cannot be reached (401 / needs login), report the reading failure and `needs_human`, naming exactly what could not be read; do not convert that downstream tool failure into a rejection of an otherwise supported pre-approval requisition. Never infer the owner’s decision from the applicant's description of the plan.

## Probation confirmation (转正申请) (Derek, 2026-09-24)

Read the probation assessment standard set when the person was hired and the record of what was actually completed. **Every standard item must correspond one-to-one to a completion record, and there must be a recorded review approval.** An item with no completion record, a completion record that answers no standard item, or no review-approval record is a material gap: return it to the submitter naming the item. Do not confirm on a general statement that the probation went well.

## Compensation change (薪酬变更申请) (Derek, 2026-09-24)

Require both a **salary-adjustment basis** and a **trial-run assessment standard**, and judge whether the change is reasonable against them and the salary evidence in the hiring section (HR table, or the market band you research yourself). Either missing is a submitted-material gap: return it. Both present but the change is not supported by them (out of band, or not tied to the assessed result) is an unmet standard: table row 8.

For performance or compensation decisions in the algorithm department, Derek personally makes the final decision. Route that reserved decision to Derek under the generic OA Skill; do not treat another approver's recommendation as the final decision.

## Org-structure change (组织架构变动申请) (Derek, 2026-09-24)

The approval must have a specific business basis, verified against available evidence such as headcount and reporting records, business need, and the affected people and work. A blank basis or a generic phrase such as “business adjustment” is not enough to approve, but it is an applicant-resolvable information gap: **No basis: request the missing business rationale from the submitter** or another verified responsible party. First check the OA identities and process roles to confirm who can answer; do not guess from a person's title alone. Ask for the concrete business need, affected work and people, receiving arrangement, and reporting line; an explicit statement in the flow by the original or the receiving supervisor counts as that confirmation, so do not ask for a separate confirmation record. Accept a substantive explanation and evidence already available in the form, comments, or linked records; do not require a particular document or format. If the facts are missing and the current OA process has a valid return path to the responsible party, propose a formal return for correction rather than leaving it in Derek's pending queue after a comment. State the exact missing fact and the evidence already checked. Comment only when no valid return path exists or the matter genuinely depends on an external party rather than applicant-supplied facts, and keep the OA pending. When a specific respondent has been verified, explicitly address that person by name in the return/comment (for example, “请吴柯欣补充……”) rather than using only “请补充” or an abstract label such as “申请责任方”; this makes the intended respondent clear without claiming that the comment mechanism itself routes only to that person. Do not escalate solely because the agent cannot independently verify facts that the submitter or responsible party can explain or substantiate. After a return/comment, independently verify any response against available records before deciding. If a response leaves a specific factual gap, request only that unresolved detail; do not repeat a question already answered in the form or comments. **Use `needs_human` only for a genuine policy or authority gap or an explicit business choice reserved for Derek**, not for missing case facts that can be clarified. Never approve or reject solely on an unsupported assertion.

An ordinary final approval decision at Derek's assigned node is not, by itself, a personal decision reserved for `needs_human`; it is the normal outcome of reviewing the application. Only an explicit discretionary choice outside the normal approval decision, or a real policy/authority gap, belongs in `needs_human`. If the business rationale or affected-work facts are missing, first ask the verified submitter or responsible party and keep the approval pending, even when Derek owns the current approval node. A previous comment asking Derek to decide does not replace asking the verified submitter or responsible party for missing case facts; ask only the missing factual question, and do not repeat an identical question to the same recipient.

For an org-structure change, reconstruct the evidence and approval history in source-timestamp order using DWS operation records and submission times for each readable, source-verified item. Only evidence submitted after a verified return can count as a post-return response for that cycle; an earlier screenshot, comment, or attachment does not become new because a later approver refers to it. Treat concrete, verifiable substantive statements in approval comments as evidence too; do not require a separate confirmation document when the comment itself states the facts. Apply the generic OA Skill's same-gap threshold: count only a verified, actionable return based on a decision-changing, applicant- or responsible-party-resolvable gap, supported by evidence readable at the time and followed by a fair chance to correct it. Before three valid returns, do not reject by count; use the ordinary approval criteria and, only while the same actionable gap remains, the next specific return needed for correction. At Derek's authorized review node after three such effective returns, reject only if current readable, source-verified evidence still proves that same substantive gap unresolved; state the gap and the three valid correction rounds. A reviewer-side permission, parsing, OCR, or client read failure; an unsupported or non-actionable request; a different gap; or a gap cured by post-return evidence does not count toward the threshold. A responsible approver's renewed approval is a decision and part of the approval history, but it neither automatically cures the gap nor replaces review of the approver's comments and other evidence. Judge on the facts: approve when the stated business need, affected work and people, receiving arrangement, and reporting relationship are supported and no material contradiction remains. Existing department and supervisor records describe the pre-change baseline, not a contradiction with the proposed post-change arrangement. Do not repeat an already settled request unless a distinct new gap appears. Use `no_action` when the task is not assigned to the authorized reviewer or the process is terminal.

Before applying the three-return threshold, use the generic OA Skill's per-return verification table. Do not use the count of DWS `REDIRECT_PROCESS` records or service receipts as a substitute for reviewing each return and its subsequent response. For every round, verify what facts were known at that time, the exact same decision-changing gap and actionable request, the timestamped response (including approver comments), and whether that response actually cured the gap. If a response cures the gap, that unresolved-gap chain ends; later returns cannot keep the resolved issue alive or count toward its threshold. Count only separately verified rounds where the same gap remained supported after the response. A lack of a new attachment is not proof that no substantive response was made.

## Shared evidence and result contract

Keep the applicant's claims separate from independently verified records. For each case report the live process identity and people-review type; full materials read/unread; applicable primary sources and their version/effective-date status; facts, evidence and relevant historical/context material; `information_completeness`, `rule_coverage`, risk and confidence; the applicable type-specific branch; action authority and mapping; applicant-resolvable question; and each policy gap requiring `needs_human`.

Do not call the category 100% covered because its evidence checklist is detailed. A case can only have `rule_coverage = 1.0` when this Skill's criteria for its exact type were each checked against the material and held. Actions follow the decision table in `$dingtalk-oa-approval`: this Skill supplies the criteria a case is judged against, and the table turns the result into approve, return, comment, reject or `needs_human`. A category not having its own action list is not a rule gap. Keep correctable material gaps separate from rule gaps and follow the generic Skill's return-first and independent-escalation rules.
