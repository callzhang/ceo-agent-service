# Stardust OA Attendance and Travel Review

Use together with `$dingtalk-oa-approval` for Stardust leave, business-travel and outing approvals. The shared category reflects evidence overlap only; do not transfer approval criteria across leave, travel and outing. The generic Skill owns cross-company evidence, scoring, workflow and DingTalk action mechanics.

Treat the applicable criteria in this Skill as the runtime rules for leave, travel and outing reviews. Do not search for or read the background OA-principles document to supply policy. Read an external policy only when this Skill explicitly requires it for a case; if such a required source is unavailable, or this Skill lacks a decision-relevant threshold, exception or authority, mark rule coverage below 1.0 and route the policy gap to `needs_human`.

## Leave

Read the leave type, dates/duration, reason and relevant schedule/handover context. Apply a duration-proportionate continuity check: a brief absence should not trigger the same handover paperwork as a multi-week absence. Judge the request in its actual context, including the applicant's intern/student status, school calendar, public holidays, travel home, and the manager's stated coverage plan. A credible, specific reason such as returning home around a major holiday together with a school requirement can support several days of personal leave; do not require a day-by-day justification or proof that each day could not be shortened unless a concrete mismatch or contrary evidence makes duration decision-relevant. When commenting, be considerate and ask only for a concrete, decision-relevant gap.

### Proportionate handover

- **Half-day leave (up to 0.5 workday):** Do not require an itemized work list, progress report, or post-leave responsibility plan when no material work is due during the absence and the manager is aware. If the OA form forces a handover node even though no substantive coverage is needed, the applicant may select themself to satisfy that workflow field; treat this only as the form's administrative node, not as evidence that another person has agreed to cover work. Do not return the request merely because the applicant is the selected person. If the schedule or known commitments show a concrete continuity risk, ask only how that specific item will be covered.
- **More than half a day and under 3 days:** Check for a practical coverage arrangement when work will need coverage. A named colleague's actual agreement in the flow is sufficient absent contrary evidence; do not demand a detailed task-by-task handover without a concrete risk that requires it.
- **3 days or more:** Keep the existing reason standard and confirm that critical work has a workable owner during the absence. A responsible manager's explicit, current staffing or allocation plan is evidence for coverage; use it rather than asking the applicant to repeat the same information or produce an exhaustive checklist. If the OA flow names a specific handover person, still verify that person's relevant capability and actual agreement in the flow. A manager's plan does not substitute for that person's required flow agreement.
- An on-call or reachable arrangement during leave is not inherently inconsistent with taking leave. Occasional online availability or meeting attendance alone does not require the applicant to choose between leave and remote work. A statement that work or meetings will be online does not, by itself, show that ordinary work is expected or justify asking whether the requested full-day leave is actually remote work; treat the submitted leave type as controlling unless the schedule or assigned duties show a concrete conflict. Do not ask the applicant to reclassify leave as remote work solely because of availability or an online-work/meeting note. Consider the actual rest period and expected response/workload; do not demand a shorter leave interval merely because the applicant remains available for occasional on-call coverage. Clarify or correct the interval only when the stated duties materially conflict with leave, the applicant is explicitly expected to perform ordinary work through the period, or the form's duration otherwise changes a material entitlement or balance.
- For a fixed academic or other dated milestone, use the stated milestone date to assess why the requested end date is needed when the relationship is clear. Do not ask again why the leave cannot end earlier when the existing request and manager's current explanation already answer that question.

Before asking for more detail or returning a leave request, check the current form, operation records, and directly linked applicant or manager statements for an answer already provided. If the same material point has already been answered and the rule is met, stop repeating the request and move to the applicable decision; reopen it only for a genuinely new fact or a material remaining risk.

Do not use this proportionality guidance to waive a material eligibility, entitlement, safety, or coverage condition. Ask only for missing facts that could change the decision.

**Discretionary leave of 3 days or more needs a reasonable, specific reason** (Derek, 2026-09-23). Count the requested days from the form, including half days, and judge reason and duration together in context. A generic reason ("个人事务", "休息", "家里有事" with nothing more) may need one focused clarification; it is not grounds to reject. Do not impose a universal requirement to prove why every day cannot be shortened or moved. Ordinary, plausible circumstances such as an intern returning home around a public holiday for a school fitness test can justify the requested span absent concrete contrary evidence. If the reason and dates remain materially inconsistent after checking the form and comments, ask only about that inconsistency; if a genuine policy or entitlement gap controls the decision, follow the policy-gap rule below.

**Exception — leave guaranteed by law or company policy (including marriage leave):** Treat the entitlement as the employee's right and review it humanely. Do not apply the discretionary 3-day test to demand that the employee justify why the entitled leave is needed, why it cannot be shortened, or why its dates cannot be moved. Instead, verify only the decision-relevant entitlement conditions: applicable jurisdiction/company rule, eligibility, duration and counting basis (workdays or calendar days), and any valid timing window or minimum evidence requirement. Request only missing facts needed to establish eligibility; do not ask for unrelated private details. Still arrange work handover where required by the workflow, but do not treat a missing discretionary-style explanation as a reason to deny an entitlement. If the applicable entitlement or a material eligibility condition cannot be established from an available authoritative rule, do not guess or apply the discretionary test as a substitute: set `rule_coverage < 1.0` and route the policy gap to `needs_human`.

Leave-type eligibility, available balance and evidence requirements for leave outside an established entitlement are not yet defined here: they block only a case that actually turns on one of them. Do not assume a missing balance/eligibility field is decision-relevant when the current form is accepted as a personal-leave request, no one raises an eligibility or balance conflict, and the review can be resolved under the applicable reason, date and continuity criteria. Escalate only when the decision actually depends on the missing field or available evidence shows a conflict.

Do not use performance-management clauses about long illness, maternity or special performance treatment as leave-approval authority; those clauses govern performance treatment unless a primary leave policy explicitly says otherwise. If policy criteria affect the decision and are unavailable/incomplete, set `needs_human` and keep `rule_coverage < 1.0`.

### Handover person — preconditions (updated by Derek, 2026-10-12)

The handover person may be another member of the applicant's team, including a superior, subordinate or peer, or a member of another team doing the same kind of work. Verify team membership or matching job function from current organizational evidence. The person must be included in this approval chain; being named only in a form or chat is insufficient. Do not require the person to be the applicant's subordinate, and do not apply that obsolete restriction to the approver. For leave of 3 days or more, the applicant cannot serve as their own handover person. For leave of up to 3 days (including a single full day), when no work needs coverage or the applicant stays reachable/on call with the manager's knowledge, the applicant may select themself for the form's handover node, exactly as in the half-day exception above; do not return the request for lacking a separate non-applicant handover person, and do not require a cover person for an occasional duty (for example periodic audit support) once the daily duties are covered by an agreeing colleague. This relationship rule does not replace the capability and agreement checks below when another person is actually covering work.

For leave longer than half a workday, and for business travel that names someone to cover the work, two things must hold **before** the request can be approved when work is actually being handed over. Both are preconditions, not factors to weigh:

1. **The handover person is capable of the work being handed over.** Look the person up (`dws contact user get --ids <userId>`) and compare their role, department and level with the work the applicant is leaving. Someone in an unrelated function, or clearly junior to work that needs judgement, does not qualify. When they do not, comment naming the mismatch and ask for a suitable handover person; do not approve.
2. **The handover person has already agreed in this approval flow.** Find their own `AGREE` in the operation records. A handover person who has not yet agreed — because the flow has not reached them, or they have not acted — means the request is waiting on someone else: do not approve; comment stating whose agreement is outstanding, and wait. Their name appearing in the form is not agreement.

If an absence of 3 days or more, or a shorter one with work genuinely due during it, creates a need for coverage and no handover person is named, comment asking for one once; do not approve. For a half-day absence with no material work due, do not demand a separate cover person or itemized handover solely because the form contains a handover field; apply the narrow workflow-field exception above when the form requires a selection.

### Review after the requested dates

Elapsed leave or travel dates are not, by themselves, a reason for no_action or skipping review. Once the requested leave dates have fully passed, do not return the request: handover and future coverage no longer exist to be corrected. Decide on the remaining facts: approve when the reason is credible and the approvers have agreed; ask by comment, not return, only for a missing fact that controls entitlement or balance. Review the actual content, applicable rules, handover and historical evidence even after the period has ended. Distinguish retrospective review from arranging future coverage: do not invent future handover or treat elapsed dates as proof of approval, rejection or completeness. Apply the normal evidence-based decision table and existing authorization; check prior correction receipts before asking again.

## Business travel

Read the current travel standard and verify purpose/customer or opportunity evidence, visit plan, itinerary/dates, budget and expected result. Compare expenses with the current applicable limits by city, level and cost type, and inspect any exception authorization. Active travel limits, budget ownership and exception approvers are not yet defined here; a trip whose expenses need a limit comparison is blocked on that limit, not on an action list.

If a correctable applicant fact or attachment is missing, ask for that item and follow generic return-first handling. If the standard, limit, exception or authority is missing, that is a separate policy gap requiring `needs_human`; do not treat a quote or applicant assurance as company policy.

## Outing

Read the purpose, location, date/time, business relevance and effect on critical work. **An outing is not business travel and is held to a much lighter standard** (Derek, 2026-09-23). Approve an outing when its business value is clear from the purpose stated. Do not apply the travel checks — standards, budget, itinerary, expense limits — to an outing. When the business value cannot be told from what is written, ask in a comment; that is not grounds to reject. Business travel below stays strict. If an uncovered boundary is material to the decision, `rule_coverage < 1.0` and `needs_human`.

## Required result and open gaps

For each case report live process identity and subtype; material and schedule/expense evidence read; source, version/effective date, scope and exact clauses; facts versus applicant assertions; `information_completeness`, `rule_coverage`, risk and confidence; applicable limits/exceptions/authority; applicant question if a factual item is missing; and each unresolved policy field requiring `needs_human`.

Known missing source fields include: current effective leave policy; active travel limits and exception matrix; outing-vs-travel classification and reasonableness boundaries; authorized decision makers; and approver authority. Actions follow the decision table in `$dingtalk-oa-approval`: this Skill supplies the criteria a case is judged against, and the table turns the result into approve, return, comment, reject or `needs_human`. A category not having its own action list is not a rule gap. Keep applicant-resolvable material gaps separate from company-policy gaps.
