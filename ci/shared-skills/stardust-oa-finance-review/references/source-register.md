## Verified excerpts, paraphrased without expanding scope

- **Expense / reimbursement:** invoices, receipt/payment proof and travel evidence are required; ordinary reimbursement is within the current month, exceptionally by next month's 20th; entertainment, transport and no-invoice cases have stated document conditions.
- **Travel:** use the trip approval once; accommodation uses a VAT special invoice; submit on the expense month or within three working days after return for cross-month travel; attach invoices, itineraries, hotel water bill and payment proof.
- **Direct payment:** payment-contract-invoice entities must be identical; attach bill and contract; recover invoice within five working days after payment, no later than the following month absent a reported exception.
- **Supplier payment:** payment depends on customer-confirmed delivery and stated settlement conditions; verify payment amount versus cost confirmation, contract validity, CRM number, and invoice/settlement/payee/contract consistency.
- **Advance:** only administrative procurement or travel; administrative advances settle/return by 31 December and travel advances are written off within five working days after the trip; DingTalk's 备用金申请 is required.
- **Revenue:** data service recognition uses customer acceptance email/form and its specified recognition point; software service uses implementation acceptance; revenue pack requires the income summary and named business, management, finance and preparer sign-off plus external customer evidence.
- **Asset disposal / special matter:** a limited source covers bad-debt provision after 36 months plus evidence of non-recovery and management approval, and a contract gross-margin special-approval condition below 25%. Neither is a full rule for its similarly named OA template.
