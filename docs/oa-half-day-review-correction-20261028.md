# Half-day leave review correction — 2026-10-28

Two reported half-day compensatory-leave reviews had handover and HR agreement.
This public record retains anonymous findings only; exact source identities and
private runtime evidence remain local.

## Diagnosis

The real worker database is resolved by `app.config.worker_db_path()`, under
`~/Library/Application Support/ceo-agent-service`, rather than checkout `data/`.
The earlier checkout-database absence finding was invalid for production.

The first Consumer proposed approval. Its first Audit inferred a balance or
eligibility gap from the compensatory-leave label. A subsequent Audit withdrew that
requirement, then the following Audit reinstated it because the candidate had not proved
the condition irrelevant. The eventual external comments asked for a policy and,
after the applicant explained compensatory leave, a half-day balance.

The second Consumer proposed approval after checking a capable same-team
handover colleague's agreement. Its Audit treated an open proxy/developer-tool
incident as outside the accepted operational handover unless separately named.
The final approved candidate requested its status and another explicit owner.

The native read_task_skill receipts from both initial Consumer/Audit session
pairs carry the same attendance digest:
`12fe5ae6a03b2903ffe92df32c18ea25e0330f20d9e202f23fdbee6ee8ded375`.
That is also the pre-change repository and installed-file digest. This is a
business-rule applicability problem, not missing Skill delivery or route drift.

## Repair

The shared attendance Skill explicitly applies the existing decision-relevance
rule to compensatory leave. A type label or missing balance field alone does not
require policy discovery or proof that no additional condition applies. Actual
applicable balance rules and concrete conflicts remain decision-relevant.
Operational coverage is judged by the accepted responsibility scope. An open
incident within that scope does not require another incident-specific handover
statement or resolution as a condition of taking leave. Concrete duties outside
the accepted scope or evidence of inability still require clarification.

No generic Audit instruction, feedback budget, task state, provider action or
authority mechanism is changed. The existing Skill publication mechanism and
normal Consumer/Audit execution path remain responsible for live application.
